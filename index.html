How to Play

Tap an arrow to extract it in the direction it points.

  • An arrow can only escape if every cell in front of its tip is clear, all the way to the edge.
  • A blocked tap costs you a ♥ — the blocking arrow flashes red.
  • Lose all 3 hearts and the level restarts.
  • Clear the entire grid to advance.
  • Stuck? Press Hint.

No timer. No pressure. Just logic.
