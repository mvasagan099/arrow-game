How to Play

Tap an arrow to extract it in the direction it points.

  • An arrow can only escape if every cell in front of its tip is clear to the grid edge.
  • A blocked tap costs you a ♥ — the blocking arrow briefly flashes.
  • Lose all 3 hearts and the level restarts.
  • Clear the entire grid to advance!
  • Pinch to zoom, drag to pan, and press Hint when stuck.

No timer. No pressure. Pure logic.
