How to Play

Your goal is to extract every arrow from the grid without collisions.

Rules

  • Click or tap an arrow to make it fly off the board in the direction it points.
  • The path must be completely clear — no other arrows may stand in its way to the edge.
  • If you pick a blocked arrow you lose a ❤️ heart. The level resets when hearts run out.
  • Clear the whole board to win. There is always at least one valid order.

Controls

  • Mouse: Left-click = remove • Right-drag = pan • Wheel = zoom
  • Touch: Tap = remove • Long-press = hint • Pinch = zoom • 2-finger drag = pan
  • Keyboard: H Hint • R Restart • U Undo • Space Reset view • Arrow keys nudge

No timers. Think ahead. Enjoy the calm.
