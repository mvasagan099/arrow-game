# Google Play Console — Content Rating & Target Audience Guide

## 1. Executive Summary

This guide assists developers in completing the **International Age Rating Coalition (IARC)** questionnaire and **Target Audience and Content** declarations in the Google Play Console for **Arrow Escape**.

Following these answers accurately ensures that the game receives an **Everyone (PEGI 3 / ESRB Everyone / USK 0)** rating across global markets.

---

## 2. IARC Content Rating Questionnaire Walkthrough

When prompted in Google Play Console (**App Content > Content rating > Start questionnaire**):

1. **Category Selection**:
   - Select: **Game**

2. **Violence**:
   - Does the game contain violence toward humans or animals? **No**
   - Does the game contain realistic or fantastical violence? **No**
   - Does the game depict blood or gore? **No**

3. **Fear / Horror**:
   - Does the game contain scary or gruesome imagery? **No**

4. **Sexuality & Nudity**:
   - Does the game contain nudity or sexual content? **No**
   - Does the game contain sexual themes or references? **No**

5. **Simulated Gambling & Wagering**:
   - Does the game contain simulated gambling (e.g. roulette, slot machines)? **No**
   - *Note on Virtual Coins*: Arrow Escape uses virtual offline puzzle coins. Because these coins cannot be purchased with real money, cannot be cashed out, and do not involve randomized casino-style wagering/loot boxes, this does NOT constitute simulated gambling.

6. **Language & Profanity**:
   - Does the game contain offensive language, profanity, or hate speech? **No**

7. **Controlled Substances**:
   - Does the game reference alcohol, tobacco, or illegal drugs? **No**

8. **User Interaction & Data Sharing**:
   - Does the game allow users to interact or exchange messages with others? **No** (Single-player only).
   - Does the game share user-provided personal info with third parties? **No**
   - Does the game share the user's current physical location? **No**

### Expected Global Rating Outcome:
- **PEGI**: PEGI 3
- **ESRB**: Everyone
- **USK**: USK 0 (Freigegeben ohne Altersbeschränkung)
- **ClassInd**: Livre (L)
- **ACB**: General (G)

## 3. Target Audience & Families Policy Declaration

Under **Google Play Console > App Content > Target audience and content**:

1. **Target Age Groups**:
   - Select: **13–15**, **16–17**, and **18 and over**.
   - Do **NOT** select age groups under 13 (5 and under, 6–8, 9–12) unless you intentionally want to enter Google's Designed for Families program.

2. **Why Target Age 13+?**:
   - Selecting ages under 13 places the application under Google Play's strict **Families Policy**, requiring compliance with COPPA, child-directed advertising SDK certification, and restricted ad formats.
   - Designating the app for general audiences (13+) allows standard AdMob ad mediation while remaining safe for all ages.

3. **Store Presence & Appeal to Children**:
   - "Could your store listing unintentionally appeal to children?"
   - Select: **No**.
   - Ensure store listing descriptions and graphics maintain a sleek, clean, modern puzzle design rather than cartoonish baby themes.
