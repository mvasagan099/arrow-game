Privacy Policy for Arrow Escape

Effective Date: November 3, 2026 | Last Updated: November 3, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Arrow Escape: Puzzle Logic ("we", "us", or "our") handles user information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing a transparent, offline-friendly mobile gaming experience.

Arrow Escape is an offline puzzle application. We do not require you to create an account, register, or provide personal information (such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number) to play the game.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The core gameplay of Arrow Escape functions entirely offline. The following gameplay data is stored strictly locally on your device within your device's sandboxed storage (localStorage):

  • Level Progression: Solved level records and star ratings across the 2,000 levels.
  • Game Economy: Virtual coin balance and transaction history (capped at 50 entries).
  • Customizations: Unlocked and equipped cosmetics (arrow skins, board styles, particle trails, and visual themes).
  • Achievements & Daily Challenges: Completed achievement timestamps and daily challenge streak records.
  • Player Preferences: Sound effects volume, haptic feedback toggle, and selected theme.

We do not transmit, sync, or store this gameplay data on any external servers. If you uninstall the application or clear your app data in Android Settings, this local data will be permanently deleted unless restored via your personal Google Drive device backup.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob & UMP)

When your device is connected to the internet, Arrow Escape integrates third-party software development kits (SDKs) provided by Google LLC to deliver advertisements and manage user consent.

3.1 Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob)

Google AdMob may collect and process certain device and usage data to serve advertisements, prevent advertising fraud, and monitor performance:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID) and standard device information (model, operating system version, carrier).
  • Network & Diagnostics: IP address, general approximate location (country/city level inferred from IP), connection type, and ad performance metrics (views, clicks, dismissals).
  • Interaction Data: Diagnostic data related to advertisement display and crash telemetry.

For more details, review Google's official privacy policies: Google Privacy Policy and Partner Sites Terms.

3.2 Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) & Consent Choices

In regions subject to regulations such as the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK GDPR, Arrow Escape uses the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to present a consent dialog prior to requesting personalized advertisements.

  • Consent Choices: You may choose to accept or refuse personalized advertising. If you decline consent or choose non-personalized ads, contextual advertisements will be served without using your Advertising ID for behavioral profiling.
  • Revoking / Changing Consent: You can modify your advertising privacy choices at any time within the game:
    1. Open the game's Settings dialog.
    2. Tap "Privacy & Ad Choices".
    3. Update your preferences via the Google UMP consent form.

4. Children's Privacy

Arrow Escape is designed for a general audience (ages 13 and older) and is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Data Security

Because we do not operate backend servers or store user data in the cloud, there are no central databases to compromise. Network communications initiated by the Google Mobile Ads SDK are encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3).

6. How to Delete Your Data

You have complete control over your local game data:

  • To reset all progress, coin balances, and unlocked cosmetics, go to Settings in the game and select "Reset Progress", or navigate to Android Settings > Apps > Arrow Escape > Storage > Clear Data.
  • To opt out of personalized advertising at the Android OS level, navigate to Settings > Google > Ads > Delete Advertising ID.
